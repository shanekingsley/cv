Who I Am

I'm Shane Kingsley, a senior engineer with over a decade of delivering solutions that others called too complex. APIs, integrations, migrations, cloud infrastructure. I've worked across the full technical stack and across multiple industries.

These days I'm most energised by solution design and high-level architecture: the point where the right technical decision changes a business outcome. In complex discussions I tend to see the whole picture early, picking up on connections and dependencies that surface for others later. It's not a party trick. It's how I'm wired.

Alongside my employed work, I take on freelance projects for small businesses, building software that genuinely fits their needs rather than forcing them into a template.
