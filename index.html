About

Who I Am

I'm a senior engineer who's spent over a decade delivering solutions that others called too complex. APIs, integrations, migrations, cloud infrastructure — I've worked across the full technical stack and across multiple industries.

These days I'm most energised by solution design and high-level architecture: the point where the right technical decision changes a business outcome. I see how systems should connect — often before anyone else in the room has framed the question.

I communicate clearly with non-technical people, deliver on what I promise, and tell the truth about what's possible. That combination is rarer than it should be.

Alongside my employed work, I take on freelance projects for small businesses — building things that genuinely fit their needs rather than forcing them into a template.
